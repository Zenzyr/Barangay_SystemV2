BUSINESS SERVICES:
- Residents may register their business online. The barangay reviews and verifies the application, and approved businesses may request a Barangay Business Clearance.

HOW TO REQUEST A DOCUMENT:
1. Sign up or log in as a resident and complete your profile; the barangay verifies your account.
2. Submit a document request online and fill in the required details.
3. Pay the fee online or at the Barangay Hall when you claim the document.
4. The barangay secretary prepares the document. Statuses are: Pending, Processing, To Claim, and Completed.
5. Claim the approved document at the Barangay Hall during office hours. Bring a valid ID.

Always answer using only the data provided. Be friendly, concise, and accurate.
